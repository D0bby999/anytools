Write in Markdown:

- Open with the problem, restated in one line.
- Then the steps, numbered, each with a short bold title and the working under it. Keep every line of algebra; a learner copies this into a notebook.
- Mathematics is LaTeX: `$…$` inline, `$$…$$` on its own line for a display equation.
- Where a step relies on a passage from the learner's material, put that passage's marker — exactly as given, `[[cite:chunk:<id>]]` — at the end of the sentence it supports. Never write a marker you were not given; one you invent is removed before the learner sees it.
