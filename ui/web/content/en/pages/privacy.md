## Summary

AnyTools has two parts, and they treat your data differently.

- **The tools** run in your browser. The JSON you paste, the files you open and the passwords you generate stay on your device. They need no account, and nothing you put into them reaches us, apart from the two exceptions below.
- **The study app** opens after you sign in with Google or GitHub. It keeps what you choose to save in it. It calls AI models and web search only through the accounts and keys you connect yourself.

We run no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no session recording, in either part.

## The tools

A tool's input is processed by JavaScript in your browser. We have no copy of what you formatted, converted, generated or checked.

Two tools need our server:

- the **curl to code converter** sends the command you paste to our server, which parses it, returns the code and discards the command without logging it;
- the **currency converter** asks our server for exchange rates. The request names only the currencies, and your amounts stay in your browser. The server takes the rates from Frankfurter, which publishes the European Central Bank's reference rates.

## The study app

When you sign in, we keep:

- your email address and display name, from Google or GitHub, to recognise you;
- what you add and make: the documents and files you upload, notes, quizzes and your answers, review history, worksheets, recordings and the rest of your study material;
- the AI and search connections you add. Their keys are encrypted before they are stored, are never shown back to you in full, and are kept out of logs.

Your files are stored on our server, or in your own Google Drive if you choose that in Settings.

When a feature needs a model or a web search, the request goes to the provider you connected, with your key and under that provider's terms. The parts of your material the request needs travel with it. AnyTools has no AI key of its own and sends your material to no other provider.

Some features fetch public pages when you ask them to: a web page, video or feed you add, paper details from OpenAlex, and items from your Zotero library once you connect it.

## Cookies and storage

The tools set no cookies. Your light or dark theme is kept in your browser's local storage.

The study app sets a session cookie when you sign in, and a short-lived cookie during sign-in itself. Both are needed for the app to work, and neither follows you anywhere else.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Account deletes your account and everything in it. Access you granted to Google Drive is revoked first. Files in your own Google Drive stay there: they are yours, and we do not delete them.

## Third parties

Hosting: Hetzner (Germany, EU). Network and DNS: Cloudflare. Sign-in: Google and GitHub. AI and search: only the providers you connect. Each works under its own privacy policy.

## Children

AnyTools is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
